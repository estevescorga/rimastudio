WHAT WE DO

DIFFERENT WAYS TO COMMUNICATE. ONE SHARED PERSPECTIVE.

We work across different areas of visual communication, bringing together complementary skills and creative approaches to find the right solution for each project. Sometimes, all it takes is an image. Other times, it means building an entire visual identity.

DESIGN

We create visual identities and communication materials that give shape to ideas and help brands and projects communicate with clarity and personality.

Visual Identity · Editorial Design · Packaging · Digital & Print Communication

ILLUSTRATION

We create original illustrations for different contexts and formats, exploring visual styles that bring character and a more personal dimension to each project.

Commercial Illustration · Editorial Illustration · Portraits · Special Projects

ANIMATION

We bring images, characters and stories into motion through 2D animation, creating content that makes communication more dynamic and engaging.

2D Animation · Motion Design · Explainer Videos · Digital Content

THE STUDIO

RIMA studio is founded by Ricardo Esteves Corga, illustrator, and Marta Oliveira, graphic designer. Together, we bring distinct but complementary practices into a shared visual language, bridging design, illustration and animation. Our collaboration is grounded in method, dialogue and a commitment to creating clear, consistent and enduring visual communication.

RIMA LAB

A SPACE FOR INDEPENDENT CREATIVE EXPLORATION

RIMA lab is where we bring together projects driven by our own creative practice and visual experimentation. Here, we develop original illustrations, graphic pieces and independent editions in a more open and exploratory space, where drawing, materials and visual expression become the starting point for creations that sit somewhere between art, design and publishing.
