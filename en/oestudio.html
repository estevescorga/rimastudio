RIMA studio is a creative studio dedicated to visual communication for brands and institutions. We work across graphic design, illustration and 2D animation, developing visual solutions that are clear, consistent and functional.

We believe visual communication begins with listening and understanding context. For that reason, every project starts from strategy, objectives and the identity of those who seek us out. Our work is grounded in method, intention and attention to detail, creating solutions designed to respond to the real needs of brands and to stand the test of time.

The studio was founded by Ricardo Esteves Corga, illustrator, and Marta Oliveira, graphic designer. Together, we bring distinct yet complementary practices into a shared visual language, bridging design, illustration and animation. Our collaboration is built on dialogue, reflection and close involvement throughout every stage of a project.

We value well-defined processes, transparent collaboration and close relationships with our clients. We accompany each project with care, ensuring coherence, clarity and real-world applicability in the everyday life of brands.

More than creating visual pieces, we aim to develop communication that works, that makes sense within its context and remains relevant over time.
