A more personal form of recognition

A company is made of many things, but above all, of the people who build it every day.

The People Series was created as a more personal and artistic way to recognise those people. An author illustration service for companies that value their employees and want to mark meaningful moments in a less conventional, more thoughtful way.

Rather than simply reproducing a photograph, we turn each person into a unique piece designed to be given, kept and associated with a moment of recognition.

Several portraits side by side (or detail of 2–3 framed portraits)

Made for meaningful moments

The People Series can be part of different moments of recognition within a company:

  • Work anniversaries
  • 5, 10, 15, 20 or more years with the company
  • Retirement
  • Employee departures
  • Recognition of a contribution or achievement
  • Internal awards
  • Company anniversaries
  • Team celebrations
  • Annual employee recognition programmes

It can work as a one-off initiative or as an ongoing recognition programme over the years.

Created by RIMA studio

Each portrait is developed by the RIMA studio team, Marta and Ricardo, using photographs and other visual references of the person being portrayed.

The work always starts from an author-led, contemporary and human illustration language, while being adapted to the specific context of each project.

Rather than simply reproducing one image, we cross-reference different materials to better understand each person's expression, features and presence.

One concept, different approaches

Each project can take on a different visual language while keeping the identity of The People Series and the author-led nature of the illustration.

The three approaches differ mainly in the way they use line, colour, shadow and texture, allowing the result to adapt to the context, scale and identity of each company.

None is presented as superior to the others. They are simply different ways of interpreting the same principle: creating recognisable, personal portraits with meaning for the person receiving them.

Line

The simplest and most essential approach.

The portrait is built mainly through line, with a very restrained use of colour. The image is reduced to the elements needed to preserve the expression, posture and most recognisable features of each person.

The result is light, clean and timeless, maintaining a strong connection to the reference while still feeling clearly illustrated.

Form

A more constructed approach, where line is complemented by areas of colour, soft shadows and background elements.

These layers add definition and presence to the portrait, creating a fuller image while preserving the simplicity and clarity of the drawing.

The result remains contemporary and balanced, with a more graphic reading and a stronger sense of depth.

Texture

The most expressive and visually rich approach.

Line is layered with colour, hatching, marks, overlays and textures, creating a portrait with greater graphic density and a more editorial character.

The portrait still preserves the identity and essential features of the person, while allowing for a freer, more dynamic and author-led interpretation.

How it works

  1. 01

    Brief

    We understand the context of the initiative, number of employees, occasion, timing and level of customisation required.

  2. 02

    References

    The company provides, ideally, at least two recent references of each person.

  3. 03

    Visual direction

    RIMA studio proposes the illustration approach we believe best fits the project. The client may choose between Line, Form or Texture, with no price difference between styles.

  4. 04

    Illustration

    Each portrait is developed individually by the RIMA team.

  5. 05

    Preview

    Before physical production, we always send a digital preview for approval. One round of minor adjustments is included.

  6. 06

    Production

    After approval, each portrait is prepared, printed and finished.

  7. 07

    Delivery

    The pieces can be delivered as prints only or framed and ready to give. A high-resolution .jpg file is always included.

FAQ

How many photographs are needed?

Ideally, at least two recent references of each person.

Do the photographs need to be professional?

No. Informal photographs can work perfectly well, as long as the person can be seen clearly.

Can you work from video?

Yes. Video frames can be used as references when they show the person clearly.

Does the client choose the style?

RIMA studio proposes the approach we believe best fits the project, but the client can choose between Line, Form or Texture. The style does not affect the price.

Is there an approval stage before printing?

Yes. We always send a digital preview before physical production.

Are revisions included?

One round of minor adjustments is included. Structural changes, a new reference, pose or illustration approach after work has started may require an additional quote.

What is the standard format?

The standard format is A5. Larger formats are available upon request.

Is the frame included?

There are two options: printed illustration or framed illustration.

Is the digital file included?

Yes. A high-resolution .jpg file is always included.

Can the packaging be customised?

Yes. The standard version uses The People Series / RIMA studio identity, but selected elements can be adapted to the company's identity.

Is there a minimum quantity?

There is no public minimum quantity. The service can be developed for one person or for larger series.

Can this be an annual programme?

Yes. The service is particularly suited to recurring recognition programmes such as 5, 10, 15 or more years of service.

Do you work outside Lisbon?

Yes. Projects outside Lisbon/Cascais and national or international shipping are evaluated case by case.
