ENTRELINHAS

Memories, reduced to the essential.

Entrelinhas turns photographs, videos and other references into personalised illustrations, built from lines, shapes and the details that truly matter.

A collection created by RIMA lab, our space for experimentation and product creation.

Discover Entrelinhas at RIMA lab

Matching Entrelinhas illustration

It doesn't start with the perfect photograph.
It starts with the right memory.

An old photograph. A frame from a video. Several different images. A place, a person or an apparently ordinary moment.

In Entrelinhas, the reference doesn't need to be perfect. What matters most is what it stands for.

We simplify the image, remove the excess and keep the essential. The result is a minimal composition that may say little to an outside viewer, yet carry an entire story for those who recognise the moment.

But some memories belong to more than one person.

One personal piece. Many possibilities.

Entrelinhas began as an RIMA lab collection, designed to turn personal memories into unique pieces.

But some memories also belong to a team, a company, a place or a project.

The concept can be adapted to different professional contexts: gifts for employees or clients, team moments, anniversaries, milestones, events or other occasions that deserve to be recorded in a more personal way.

Each project can be shaped around the story, the number of pieces and the context in which it will be used.

ENTRELINHAS LIVE

The same eye, now live.

Entrelinhas Live brings the concept of the collection to events and in-person experiences.

A more immediate interpretation of Entrelinhas, created in the moment. People, gestures and small scenes become minimalist illustrations that guests can take home.

More than live sketching, it is a way of creating a memory of the event itself while it is happening.

Short looping video: hand drawing → person → drawing → handover

For events, brands and teams.

Entrelinhas Live can be part of many kinds of experience:

Corporate events, brand activations, team celebrations, launches, client meetings or other moments where it makes sense to offer something created especially for those present.

The format, number of illustrations and visual approach can be adapted to each event.
